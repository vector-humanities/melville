# The leaves of the *Billy Budd* manuscript

**Houghton Library, Harvard University.** Herman Melville papers, 1761-1964 (MS Am 188-188.6), Box 19, Volume 2: *Billy Budd*. A.Ms.; [n.p., n.d.], **MS Am 188 (363)**.

Reproduced with the permission of the Houghton Library, Harvard University, granted by Leslie A. Morris, Gore Vidal Curator of Modern Books and Manuscripts, on 30 October 2026, for non-commercial research and teaching.

- Finding aid: https://hollisarchives.lib.harvard.edu/catalog/hou00338_hou00338c00388
- Harvard's digital copy: https://nrs.harvard.edu/URN-3:FHCL.HOUGH:4686413
- Harvard's IIIF manifest: https://nrs.harvard.edu/URN-3:FHCL.HOUGH:4686413:MANIFEST:3

Each leaf the Melville Electronic Library transcribes is held here twice, made from the leaf's image in Harvard's IIIF image service:

- `<leaf>.jpg`, 560 pixels wide, shown beside the text in Book;
- `<leaf>-large.jpg`, about 1,600 pixels on its long side, for the enlarged view.

`<leaf>` is MEL's identifier. MEL's `img_N` is sequence N in Harvard's manifest (the same photograph, at the same size), and `img_Nb` is a second region of sequence N's photograph.

`provenance.csv` (and `provenance.json`) give, for every file:

- the archival location, with Houghton's folio and Harvard's sequence;
- Harvard's image service, full image, page (canvas), manifest and digital object;
- the finding aid;
- the original's size in pixels;
- MEL's copy of the image and MEL's transcription;
- the credit, the permission, and the date the file was made.

`tools/leaves.py` makes the files and the record from Harvard's manifest.
